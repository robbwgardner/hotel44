Flight orders

File H-44 · Vang Vieng strip · 0410 hrs

Aircraft: C-123K, tail Hotel 44. Two radials, two jet pods. Crew of three, no names.

Cargo: 900 kilos, bundled as hard rice. It is not rice.

Deliver to Tan Son Nhut, Saigon. A Corsican buyer meets the aircraft at the far end of the field. Payment on delivery.

The Trail runs between you and the buyer. Gun sites are pencilled on your chart. Fuel is enough for half the trip — land at Savannakhet, Pakse or Ban Me Thuot to top up.

Stay out of Cambodia. Stay out of the North. If anything goes wrong, this flight never happened.
